    How this forecast is made

    Rivers and basins. Traced from the Copernicus 90 m DEM: every river of Nepal draining at least 20 km², 4,200 reaches. The traced drainage areas match 105 of the 112 published DHM stations within 10%.

    Rain. Hourly forecasts of ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS and DWD ICON (through Open-Meteo), on a 0.25° grid, averaged over the catchment above each river reach. Updated every 2 hours.

    Discharge. The rainfall-based methods used in Nepal: SCS curve number with the SCS unit hydrograph (main method), the Rational method, and the Mononobe-Rational and B.D. Richards daily peaks, over the normal flow of the month (WECS/DHM 1990).

    Flood levels. The 2- to 100-year floods of the area-based regional methods: WECS/DHM 1990, DHM 2004, MHSP 1997, WECS 2019 specific discharge and Modified Dickens (median of the methods).

    New to the page? The user guide explains the map, the five model views, the river panel and the chart, with screenshots.

    Experimental research product. These are computer estimates made automatically from weather-model rain and empirical formulas that have not been checked against measured floods at these rivers. They are not warnings. Follow the official warnings of the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (hydrology.gov.np) and local authorities.
