## 6. The ERA5 rainfall climatology (MWI, AWI)

ERA5 2019-2023 annual rain at the 0.25 deg cell of some DHM stations, against their approximate long-term normals. ERA5 is close on the plains but **too wet over the hills and mountains** (a known ERA5 bias in the Himalaya). MWI and AWI enter only the monthly-flow formulas (the base flow): with WECS/DHM 1990 (MWI^0.25-0.39) a two-fold excess raises the base flow by about 20-30%; DHM 2004 (AWI^0.47-0.76) is more affected. A gauge-based climatology (DHM normals, APHRODITE or CHIRPS) written into `prepare_cache/climate.npz` and `nepal-forecast prepare network` would correct it.
